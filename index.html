🎯 Inspection & Precision — Every Match

00 Equipment Inspection 20 pts

Pre-match Referee checks that robot + all equipment fits completely in ONE launch area and under 12 in (305 mm) height.

ConditionPts
Fits in one launch area, under height20
Otherwise0
How to solve:
  • Design compact robot + stack attachments vertically but under 12in.
  • Test with a cardboard gauge box.
  • You still start with equipment split across two launch areas — the 20pts is just for passing the one-area check.

🌳 All 15 Missions — Detail

01 Drone Survey 30 pts
Bags 1, 2

Story: Launch drone high above canopy to create LiDAR map.

ScoringPts
Drone no longer touching mat20
Bonus: LiDAR map completely flipped, scan marker at least partly in survey area+10
How it’s made to be solved:
  • Push/launch bar to flick drone off mat.
  • Second mechanism (arm/lever) flips LiDAR map tile over.
  • Map must land with scan marker in survey circle — low friction; aim for a controlled flip, not a smash.
  • Easy starter mission — close to launch area.
02 Exploding Seeds 30 pts
Bag 3

Story: Unique trees propel seeds to new ground.

ScoringPts
Each seed no longer touching stalk (×3)10 each
How it’s made to be solved:
  • Hit/bump seed pod to release seeds — push, pull or quick tap.
  • Seeds are loose; ensure they scatter off the stalk model.
  • Reliable: drive into model and reverse.
03 Flip the Rock 30 pts
Bags 4, 5

Story: Discover creatures living underneath; leave no trace.

ScoringPts
Research flag down20
Bonus: Rock returned to original starting position+10
How it’s made to be solved:
  • Mechanism to flip or push rock over to reveal flag, then push flag down.
  • For bonus, push rock back — needs precise reverse or second arm.
  • Too fast = flag not staying down. Controlled push is key.
04 Lucky Leaves 30 pts
Bag 6 🚫 No Equipment Touch end

Story: Katydid camouflages with leaves — collect leaves without disturbing it.

ScoringPts
One leaf completely removed, not touching nest10
Bonus: Second leaf also removed + katydid still in original start pos.+20 (total 30)

⚠️ If katydid is outside leaf habitat (nest + marked mat area) at end — even partly — whole mission scores 0. If katydid ever completely removed during match, bonus unavailable.

How it’s made to be solved:
  • Gentle fork/rake attachment to slide leaves out sideways.
  • Must NOT knock katydid — slow, low force.
  • Novice tip: go for 1 leaf (10pts) safely rather than risking katydid.
05 Reaching Roots 20 pts
Bag 7 🤝 Coop-related

Story: Plant stretches roots across forest boundary for symbiosis.

Scoring (only one)Pts
Plant root partially extended10
Plant root completely extended20
How it’s made to be solved:
  • Push/pull slider to extend root across line.
  • Must fully extend for 20pts — check that model clicks/locks.
  • Pairs with M07 Humongous Fungus for shared bonus (see M07).
06 Leafcutter Frenzy 40 pts
Bags 8,9,10

Story: Guide ants back to nest with leaf fragments.

ScoringPts
Each leaf fragment contained in nest with ant touching nest (×4)10 each

Fragments count only if ant is touching nest at end. Up to 4 fragments.

How it’s made to be solved:
  • Push ant model toward nest; collect fragments and drop/spill them inside.
  • Attachment with tray or plow to sweep fragments without scattering.
  • Move too fast = fragments scatter — go slow, steady push.
07 Humongous Fungus 30 pts
Bag 10 🤝 Coop — needs opponent

Story: Mycelium network connects to neighboring tree root.

ScoringPts
Mycelium completely extended20
Bonus: Connection formed with opponent’s fully extended plant root (M05). Both teams get bonus if both fully extended. Not possible in remote/no opponent.+10
How it’s made to be solved:
  • Pull/slide to extend mycelium — simple linear action.
  • Coordinate pre-match with opponent? Both need full extension for bonus — friendly coopetition.
  • Models don’t need to touch — just both fully extended = connection.

14 Seeds of Renewal 40 pts
Bag 21 🔀 Dock: Mine/City/Farm

Story: Plant diversity — place collected seeds in soil.

ScoringPts
Each seed contained within replantation station (×4)5 each
Bonus: Each of those seeds also touching mat+5 each

4 seeds max → 20 + 20 bonus = 40.

How it’s made to be solved:
  • Collect seeds from other missions (e.g., M02 / M09) and deliver to station.

Bags 22,23,24 🔀 Dock — Environmental Bonus

ScoringPts
Nesting canopy raised10

🧭 Strategy Tips

For Beginners (aim 120-200 pts)

Focus on 5-6 easy reliable missions: 01 Drone (30) + 02 Seeds (30) + 08 Tangled (30) + 11 Window (20) + 05 Roots (20) + 10 Microhabitats (20) + Inspection (20) + keep 5 tokens (50) = ~220 without rush.

Use SPIKE app Guided Mission (Competition Ready Unit) as starting program.

For Advanced (aim 350+ pts)

Chain missions by location: Run 1 (north): 08+09+05+07. Run 2 (south): 03+04+06. Run 3 (docks): 13+14+15. Keep all 6 tokens. Master funnel for M14 and gentle rake for M04.

Don’t forget the dock placement — decide Mine/City/Farm before match (RGR p7).

Rules to Remember

  • Robot is autonomous outside Home — no remote.
  • Launch: robot + anything it’ll move must fit completely inside launch area.
  • Never hand anything between left/right Home by hand (RGR Rule 14).
  • Don’t separate Dual Lock or break models — zero points.
  • End of match: stop robot, hands off — scoring starts.
